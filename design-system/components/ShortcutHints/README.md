The footer row of keycap-plus-verb hints, and the Keycap itself.

**Anatomy.** A 1px `hairline` rule above, `space-10` top padding, gaps of `space-6` (rows) and `space-14` (columns). A hint is a keycap and a `control` caption in `text-muted`, `space-5` apart. A keycap is `keycap` (11/14, sans), padded `space-1` / `space-5`, 1px `frame`, `radius-key`, in `text-control`. Enter, the arrows and ↑↓ are 12px Lucide icons inside the keycap. `.is-end` pushes a hint to the right (Esc in Palette).
